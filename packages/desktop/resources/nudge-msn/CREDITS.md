# MSN Messenger Knock wink

## Fart Guy (Gordinho peidão)

Additional original wink recovered from `Winks/Fart Guy.MCO` in the community
archive linked by santi13 on Escargot. The inner CAB contains `fartguy.swf`,
`fartguy.png`, and the Messenger content manifest. Converted from its original
139 frames at 16 fps, preserving alpha and embedded sounds (8.6875 seconds).
This is not reconstructed from the silent GIF or a screen recording.

Archive announcement:
https://www.escargot.chat/services/msn/forums/110002000195700/varius-winks-and-meego/

Source archive:
https://storage.levelleap.com/nina/clients/msnp/winks/various-winks-santi13.zip

This folder also includes the other 14 winks from the same MSN Messenger 7.0
collection. Their original SWF filenames, dimensions, durations, frame rates,
and audio presence are recorded in catalog.json. The existing knock.webm is
unchanged. New winks use transparent overlays and do not freeze the desktop.

Source collection:
https://github.com/romulodm/modern-live-messenger/tree/main/public/assets/winks

The hand animation and knocking sample are Microsoft MSN Messenger assets.
They are not covered by Backspace's source code license.

Source SWF:
https://github.com/romulodm/modern-live-messenger/blob/main/public/assets/winks/knock/knock.swf

Context identifying these as the MSN Messenger 7.0 original winks:
https://romulodm.dev/en/blog/msn-revival

Converted locally using JPEXS Free Flash Decompiler 26.4.0 and FFmpeg.
58 frames at 24 fps, original transparency; original sound triggers at frames
21, 26 and 31. Packaged as VP9 alpha + Opus WebM, with no Flash runtime.
